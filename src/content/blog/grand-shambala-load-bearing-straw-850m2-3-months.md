This video follows the construction of **Grand Shambala** at LAOM: an 850 m² **load-bearing straw** building on two levels, raised in **3 months**.

It shows the main stages of the build: foundations, base walls, straw bale walls, framing and the south facade. And how a collective build and eco-construction techniques let us move fast, with simple and reversible materials.

It is now the heart of the LAOM coliving. For the full story of the build, from the first idea to the lime plasters, read [the Grand Shambala story](/en/grand-shambala/).
